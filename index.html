Wisconsin Trail Pass

You must obtain a trail pass to be able to ride on WI trails or may be fined by law enforcement. Please support your local clubs to obtain a discounted pass.

Join a Club and become a member with your local club and a member of The Association of Wisconsin Snowmobile Clubs - AWSC

How to Get Your Trail Pass

  • Once you receive your membership number, you can order your $10 WI trail pass online through the AWSC website with a credit/debit card (MasterCard, Visa or Discover).
  • Call the AWSC Office during business hours (8 AM to 4 PM) to order your WI Trail Pass with a credit/debit card.
  • Mail through the US Post Office along your check or credit card information to the AWSC office.

Requirements

All Wisconsin recreational safety students are required to obtain a Wisconsin DNR Customer ID Number before the completion of any recreational safety class.

Students must provide their customer ID number along with personal information to the instructor.

Get Your DNR Customer ID:

  • Call DNR customer service: 1-888-936-7463 (7 AM to 10 PM)
  • Visit a DNR service center
